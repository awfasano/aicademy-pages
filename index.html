A few good questions

Before you jump in.

Is version 3 available yet?

Version 3 has been submitted for App Review and is coming soon. The download buttons open the current released app. We’ll update this page when the new version is publicly available.

Can I try AIcademy for free?

The app is free to download with a limited free AI allowance. Optional monthly and annual Premium plans give you more AI access. Check the app for current prices and allowances before subscribing.

What AP classes will the update support?

Version 3 starts with original practice for AP Biology, AP Calculus AB, and AP U.S. History. The starter library includes six five-question mini-tests and 25 flashcards. These are small practice resources, not complete courses or official exam questions.

Can I trust every AI answer?

AI can make mistakes. Use explanations to work through a topic, and check important answers against your class notes, teacher’s guidance, and course resources.

Where can I get help?

Visit AIcademy support for account, subscription, and app questions. You can also read our privacy policy.
