AIcademy Privacy Policy

Updated September 20, 2026. Version 3.0 features are identified below.

What AIcademy collects

AIcademy, operated by Anthony Fasano, helps students create and review study material. Information saved to an account is linked to that account.

  • Account details: name, email address and account identifier.
  • Study material: text and photos you submit, questions, AI replies, saved notes, cards, quizzes and coaching attempts.
  • Study progress: answers, missed questions, completed sessions and saved position, used to resume and personalize review.
  • Purchases: subscription products, transaction history and entitlement status. Apple processes payment; AIcademy does not receive your payment card details.
  • Service records: request times, usage totals, retry identifiers and error information needed to enforce allowances, prevent duplicate requests, secure accounts and troubleshoot the service. Firebase services also process IP addresses, technical device and SDK information, and diagnostics for reliability and security. Device-attestation information helps check that requests come from the app; it is not used by AIcademy for advertising.

AI processing and your choice

When you request AI generation, chat, grammar help or coaching, the selected text, photos, relevant study material and conversation context are sent through our Firebase backend to OpenAI to produce a response. Avoid submitting private information about yourself or other people that is unnecessary for studying.

Version 3.0 asks for permission before the first AI request for an account on that device. You can decline and use available saved or built-in study material. Selecting a photo does not grant access to your entire photo library.

Version 3.0 requests that OpenAI not save responses as retrievable application state. This is not a promise of zero retention: OpenAI may retain abuse-monitoring data, ordinarily for up to 30 days, and longer for legal or safety reasons. OpenAI says API content is not used for model training by default. See OpenAI’s API data controls.

Services that process information

  • Google Firebase: authentication, cloud storage, account and study records, backend processing and security checks.
  • OpenAI: the study content and context needed for the AI request you choose to make.
  • Apple and RevenueCat: transactions, account identifiers and purchase history for Premium access, restoration, fraud prevention and subscription reporting.

These services may process information outside your country. Version 3.0 does not use your study material for advertising and does not include a Facebook sign-in or advertising SDK.

Shared libraries and study packs

In version 3.0, joining a class library or contributing a pack makes the contributed content and contributor information available to members of that library. Treat an invite code as private. Anyone given the code may be able to join. Only share material you are allowed to share.

Members may copy or export material. Removing content or deleting your account cannot recall copies already saved by someone else. Public-deck features, where available, make published content available to other app users.

Storage and deletion

Saved account and study records generally remain until you remove them or request account deletion. Some local records have limits: free-response coaching retains the most recent 30 completed attempts and one draft per prompt, replacing older entries. The app also keeps local copies of some material and progress for offline use. Original source photos and text are retained on your device only if you choose to keep them; generated study material and progress can sync with your account. Signing out hides account-specific material. Retained local files stay on that device until you remove them or delete the account.

Version 3.0 keeps a private response cache available for retry recovery for up to one hour and request receipts for 24 hours. Expired records are scheduled for deletion; physical removal can occur later. Operational service logs and service-provider records follow their applicable retention policies.

Use Profile → Delete Account to request deletion. You may need to sign in again. Version 3.0 immediately restricts access and performs cloud and subscription-customer cleanup in the background, retrying temporary failures. A limited deletion record is retained temporarily to prevent delayed requests from restoring removed data.

Deleting your AIcademy account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage or cancel it in your Apple account’s subscription settings. Apple may retain transaction records under its own policies.

Your choices and support

You can choose which photos and text to submit, decline AI processing, remove saved material using available app controls, leave shared libraries, and request account deletion. Contact us for help accessing, correcting or deleting your information. Include your account email, but do not send passwords or payment details.

Privacy and support: support@theaistudytool.com.
